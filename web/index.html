How TerraTwin works

A portfolio, not a single best match.

  1. Requirements. Each base is broken into 14 testable needs, from extreme cold and polar light to hyperaridity, thin air and Jezero-like lakebeds.
  2. Scores. 71 Earth sites are scored 0–1 per need, using NASA POWER climate data and cited geology tags.
  3. Greedy portfolio. Sites are added one at a time by the largest gain in weighted coverage, minus a reachability penalty, until k sites or no real gain.
  4. Gap list. What no Earth site can test (gravity, vacuum, cosmic radiation, the lunar night) is always shown and never scored.
